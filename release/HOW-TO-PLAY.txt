QUARANTINE - co-op tactics in the infected zone

START
  Windows: double-click Quarantine.exe. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC",
           click "More info" then "Run anyway" (the game isn't code-signed yet).
  Mac:     drag Quarantine.app somewhere (e.g. Applications). The first time, RIGHT-click it and
           choose Open, then Open again. If macOS says it "is damaged" or can't be opened, run this in
           Terminal once:   xattr -cr /path/to/Quarantine.app
  Linux:   run ./Quarantine.x86_64

PLAY TOGETHER ONLINE
  One person clicks "Host online game". Everyone else types the host's address and clicks Join.
  The host's lobby shows the address to share. On the same Wi-Fi that address just works.
  Over the internet the host needs to forward port 24680 on their router, or everyone installs
  Tailscale (free) and uses the host's Tailscale address. The host clicks "Start mission".

CONTROLS
  Click a soldier, then click a highlighted tile to move.
  1 Shoot (Enter fires)   2 Overwatch   3 Hunker   4+ abilities and mutations   Esc cancel
  WASD pan, Q/E rotate, mouse wheel zoom. Press READY when you're done; enemies move once
  every player is ready.
