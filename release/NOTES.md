## What to test in this build

**Colony Security.** Act 2 and 3 fights can include Harrow's own guards, who are not Infected.
- **Shieldman:** plated and shielded; a shield blocks the hit entirely. Its slam knocks you around.
- **Overwatcher:** a sharp shot that is not slowed by your reactions.
- **Emplacement:** a fixed gun that turns toward you and sweeps a crossfire cone. Stay out of the cone.

**Automata.** Act 3 machines that run on a Relay.
- **Hound:** fast drone that hunts the weakest soldier.
- **Loader:** heavy frame that charges and slams.
- **Relay:** an antenna that gives machines nearby an extra action. Kill it and they stall to one action.

**Scavengers.** Act 1 and 2 fights can now include human looters who are not Infected. Their hits deal damage only, and the Infected walk through their fire.
- **Cutter:** its claw lifts your best item off the soldier it hits, then it runs for the map edge. Kill it and the item comes back. If it escapes, the item is gone for the rest of that fight.
- **Torchman:** lobs firebombs onto a 3x3 (2 damage, then the ground burns for 2 rounds). Shoot it and its fuel tank bursts into flames where it falls.
- **Fence:** hangs back with a sack of loot and leaves the map from turn 6, sooner if a soldier gets close. Killing it pays 25 Supplies.

**Spores now hurt enemies.** The Vector's Vent cloud and Overgrowth spores used to do nothing to the Infected. A cloud a soldier lays now deals 2 damage to enemies standing in it, and they steer around it. Spores from maps and the Source still spare them.

**Still in this build (v0.23.0).** Nearly every item, perk, mutation and graft that used to be "+1 to something" now does something you can see. Marked enemies are far easier to spot, and the Brood Mother throws rocks when she erupts.

Download the zip for your computer, unzip, and run. Mac and Windows will warn that the game is unsigned; see HOW-TO-PLAY.txt inside the zip.
