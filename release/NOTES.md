## What to test in this build

**Link items (18 new gear pieces).** Effects now feed each other, so the right pair multiplies instead of adding. Look for rare pieces in rewards and depots. They take a status in, put one out, or spread it: Mark spreading and echoing between enemies, fire turning into Marks, Suppress igniting, overwatch firing at anything that gets Marked, reloads that Mark, burn or ready you, and kills that cut your signature cooldown. Mix tags and see what compounds.

**Reactions with no gear needed.** Some status pairs now do something on their own: overwatch aims better at Marked enemies, Suppressed enemies take extra damage up close, and a Burning enemy in smoke sets the tiles around it on fire.

**Survival on set bonuses.** Burn, Mark and Suppress sets now cut damage from enemies carrying that status, Strain cuts damage from the Infected, and Close and Overwatch kills heal 1 HP. A lone soldier with one tag should hold up better.

**Also in this build:** ammo and reloading (magazines, Reload action, mag gear), reload sounds, Hale and Source three-phase fights.
