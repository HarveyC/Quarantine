; Act 1 biome "The Wall": the cordon wall and the refugee camps behind it.
; Read by MapGen (mapgen/map_gen.gd). See docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, "Tactical map generator".
;
; Parcels are 4x4 blocks:  @<role> <name>  followed by 4 rows.
;   .  floor    #  wall (full cover, blocks LOS)    =  low cover (half cover)
;   ?  50% low cover, else floor
;   P  soldier spawn (deploy parcels)    E  enemy spawn (pod parcels)
;   O  objective cell: evac / beacon / civilian / nest / hive, by the parcel's role
;   Height (see Grid.read_glyph): three levels, ground (0), roof (1), upper roof (2).
;   ^  roof floor (L1)    %  low cover on a roof    ~  fragile roof (scaffold/catwalk: explosives
;      collapse it)    2  upper roof (L2)    $  low cover on an upper roof
;   /  ladder on the ground (climbs to the roof beside it, costs 1 extra move)
;   s  stairs on the ground (walks up to the roof beside it, a normal step)
;   |  ladder on a roof (up to L2)    S  stairs on a roof (up to L2)
;   e  enemy spawn on a roof (pod parcels)
;   Destruction (see Destruction): walls break in 2 hits (to a rubble heap, then floor), roofs drop a
;   level per 2 hits. !  fuel barrel (half cover that explodes; a map with none gets up to 2 near
;   pods)    H  reinforced wall (never breaks; the map edge never breaks either)
;   Anyone can drop off a ledge (1 move per level, one way), so roofs never strand a unit, but the
;   way UP needs stairs or a ladder. Keep each roof joined to its way up inside the parcel.
;
; Edge walls (thin walls on the borders between cells, XCOM-style; see Grid.EDGE_*): draw the parcel
; at double resolution, 9 rows of 9 characters (17x17 for a landmark). Cells sit at odd positions,
; the borders between them in the gaps, '+' at the corners:
;     + +-+-+ +        - | #  wall (blocks movement and sight, full cover, 2 hits to breach)
;      .|. .o.         o      window (half cover, see-through, climbing through costs 1 extra move)
;     + + + + +        = :    low wall / railing (half cover, see-through, vaulting costs 1 extra)
;      .|= .|.         d      doorway (open: no cover, nothing blocked)
;     + +d+-+ +        space  nothing
;      . . . .
;     ...
; Capital D is a shut door: it blocks sight and costs 1 extra move to open; the first
; unit through opens it for good (it becomes d). Compound parcels use it on their street doors.
; The outer ring is the parcel's rim (shared with the next parcel or lane). Rooms need a doorway
; or a window, and no doorway should open onto a crate. Use edge walls for buildings, rooms,
; blast walls (T-walls) and railings on roofs; keep full '#' tiles for solid mass (containers,
; the cordon wall), never as lone pillars. Plain 4-row parcels still work (no edge walls).
; Roles: deploy, breach (the cordon wall, always on row 1, only mirrored left-right),
;        pod, filler, tower (high ground), landmark (8x8), evac, beacon, rescue, nest, hive.
;
; Plots lay parcels out for a whole map:  @plot <objectives> <name> lanes=<0|1>
; then one row of slot letters per parcel row, top to bottom. The drop zone is always at the bottom.
;   D deploy   B breach   E pod   F filler   T tower   O objective parcel for the fight's objective
;   L landmark: a 2x2 block of L slots filled by one 8x8 "@landmark" parcel, centred in that block,
;     the map's big multi-level building that both sides fight over
; Parcels always get open streets between them at least MapGen.MIN_LANES (2) tiles wide, so a 4x4
; plot is 22x22 tiles; lanes= only matters above that. Boss plots skip this and use lanes= as
; written (5x5 with lanes=0 is 20x20).
